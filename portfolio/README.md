## How to evaluate my work

For each future lab I plan to add the **date**, **environment**, **steps performed**, **anonymized evidence**, **expected vs actual result**, and **lessons learned**. Until then, the material here should be treated as a study guide, not as proof of implementation.

## Safety and ethics

Only use systems you own or are authorized to test. Never commit credentials, company/customer data, raw production logs, real public IP addresses from private investigations, or proprietary detections. Screenshots should be scrubbed before publication.
